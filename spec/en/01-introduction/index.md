# 01. Introduction

This chapter establishes what RUML is for and how the rest of the specification is to be read: which
documents it governs, what its normative language means, and what an implementation must do to call
itself conformant.

- [1.1 Scope](1-1-introduction.md) — What RUML encodes, and the problems it addresses
- [1.2 Document Conventions](1-2-document-conventions.md) — RFC 2119 keywords (MUST, SHOULD, MAY)
  and notation
- [1.3 Terminology](1-3-terminology.md) — The terms used throughout, from node and LexID to modular
  node
- [1.4 Conformance](1-4-conformance.md) — What a conformant document, parser, and producer must
  satisfy
- [1.5 Relationship to Other Standards](1-5-relationship-to-other-standards.md) — How RUML relates
  to JSON Schema, JSONata, and legal markup formats
